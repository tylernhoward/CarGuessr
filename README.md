# CarGuessr

A Vue 3 + TypeScript + Vite car-ID trainer, modeled after playcardle.com - guess the make, model, and year of a zoomed-in car photo sourced from Bring a Trailer.

## Why

Playcardle is great for training your eye to identify cars, but it only gives you one car a day. This is the same idea without the daily limit - hit refresh and get a new car whenever you want to keep practicing.
